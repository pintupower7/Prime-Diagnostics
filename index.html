Our Services

Comprehensive Diagnostic Solutions

We offer a full spectrum of diagnostic services using cutting-edge technology to ensure accurate and timely results for better health outcomes.

Obstetric Ultrasound

Comprehensive pregnancy scans including NT scan, anomaly scan, growth scan, and doppler studies with detailed fetal assessment.

  • 3D/4D Imaging Available
  • Fetal Echo & NT Scans
  • Biophysical Profile

Abdominal Ultrasound

Detailed imaging of liver, gallbladder, kidneys, pancreas, and spleen to detect stones, tumors, and organ abnormalities.

  • Upper & Lower Abdomen
  • KUB & Pelvic Scans
  • Prostate & Scrotal USG

Gynecological Scan

Specialized scans for ovarian cysts, fibroids, PCOS, endometrial thickness, and fertility assessment with complete confidentiality.

  • TVS & Transabdominal
  • Follicular Monitoring
  • Hysterosonography

Neurosonography

Advanced cranial ultrasound for neonates and infants to assess brain structure, ventricles, and detect congenital anomalies.

  • Through Anterior Fontanelle
  • Doppler Studies
  • Neurodevelopmental Tracking

Musculoskeletal USG

High-resolution imaging for joint effusions, tendon tears, ligament injuries, and soft tissue masses with dynamic assessment.

  • Shoulder & Knee Joints
  • Tendon & Ligament Scans
  • Guided Injections

Echocardiography

Complete cardiac evaluation including 2D Echo, Color Doppler, TTE, and stress echo for comprehensive heart health assessment.

  • 2D & 3D Echocardiography
  • Color Doppler Studies
  • Pediatric Echo
